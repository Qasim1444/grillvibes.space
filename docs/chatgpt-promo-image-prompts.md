
Brand mood:

```text
Fast, organized, reliable, profitable, modern, restaurant-focused.
```

Suggested colors:

```text
Charcoal black, warm red, flame orange, clean white, soft gray, small green success accents.
```


## Prompt Tips

- Ask for realistic device mockups when selling to serious business owners.
- Use one clear headline per image.
- Use short feature strips instead of long paragraphs inside the image.
- Generate separate images for each feature instead of forcing every feature into one design.
- For paid ads, prefer clean faces, clear device screens, and simple benefit text.
- For website banners, use wide 16:9 images with room for text.
- For Instagram, use square or 4:5 vertical images.

## Do Not Include

```text
No Apple, Google, Meta, or third-party logos unless you own rights to use them.
No fake payment provider logos.
No copied restaurant brand names.
No messy unsafe kitchens.
No exaggerated income claims.
No text like "guaranteed 10x profit".
```
